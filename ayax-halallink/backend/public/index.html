Terms & Conditions & Privacy Policy

1. Purpose & Shariah Compliance

Ayax HalalLink is strictly dedicated to facilitating lawful (Halal) marriage and honorable matrimonial introductions in accordance with Islamic principles. Casual dating, explicit content, or un-Islamic interactions are strictly prohibited.

2. Eligibility & Account Integrity

  • You must be at least 18 years of age and legally eligible for marriage under applicable laws.
  • All profile information, including age, marital status, location, and religious practice, must be truthful and accurate.
  • Creating duplicate, fake, or impersonation accounts will result in immediate and permanent suspension.

3. Guardian (Waliyyi) Oversight & Safety

To uphold modesty, transparency, and family protection, female members are strongly encouraged to link a Wali (Guardian) to oversee communications. Ayax HalalLink reserves the right to review reported conversations to enforce Islamic etiquette and prevent abuse.

4. Code of Conduct & Zero-Tolerance Policy

  • Harassment, hate speech, financial solicitation, fraud, or sharing inappropriate media is strictly forbidden.
  • Members must respect boundaries, photo-blur privacy settings, and communication preferences at all times.

5. Points, Rewards & Financial Transactions

Promotional bonus points (such as the 100 welcome points) are subject to platform verification rules. Withdrawals or paid features processed via supported gateways (Paystack or Stripe) are subject to standard identity and fraud-prevention checks.

6. Privacy & Data Protection

Personal records, private messages, and blurred profile photos are encrypted and protected. Ayax HalalLink will never sell or rent your personal data to third-party advertisers.

7. Account Termination

You may delete your account and associated profile data at any time through your account settings. Ayax HalalLink reserves the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these Terms & Conditions without prior notice.
